## Where are theme settings?

1. In the Shopify admin, open **Online store → Themes**
2. On the theme you want to customize, click **Customize**
3. In the theme editor, open **Theme settings** (the gear icon) at the bottom of the left sidebar

Changes made in theme settings apply store-wide (every page). Page-specific content is configured in "sections" → [Section reference](/en/features/sections/sg-media-hero)

## SUGOI's setting groups

Beyond Shopify's standard settings (logo, colors, typography, and so on), theme settings include SUGOI-specific groups.

| Setting group | What it covers | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme license** | Register the store using the theme (one purchase, one store) | [Details](/en/theme-settings/license) |
| **AI chat integration** | AI chat connection, privacy, measurement | [Details](/en/theme-settings/chat) |
| **Gift & noshi** | Noshi, wrapping, and name input on the product page | [Details](/en/theme-settings/gift) |
| **Purchase guidance** | Shipping, payment, and returns guidance under the buy button | [Details](/en/theme-settings/purchase-confidence) |
| **Product info for search** | Providing returns and shipping information to Google and other engines | [Details](/en/theme-settings/structured-data) |
| **Photos & spacing** | Toggle the editorial layout | [Details](/en/theme-settings/editorial) |
| **Favorites & color chips / Icons & motion** | Display details | [Details](/en/theme-settings/appearance) |

## Shopify's standard settings

Standard settings for logo, colors, typography, buttons, cards, cart, social media, and more work the same as in Shopify's official guides. See ["Customize theme settings"](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/customize-theme/edit-theme-settings) in the Shopify Help Center.

<Note>
After changing settings, don't forget **Save** in the top-right corner of the theme editor. The preview updates in real time, but nothing reaches the live store until you save.
</Note>
