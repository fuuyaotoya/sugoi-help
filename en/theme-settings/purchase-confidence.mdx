## What purchase guidance is

A feature that shows compact guidance on **shipping costs, payment methods, and the return policy** directly beneath the buy button on the product page. It resolves the "things I want to check before buying" anxieties on the spot and reduces purchase hesitation.

If there is nothing to show at all, it hides itself automatically.

In the theme editor, open **Theme settings → Purchase guidance**.

## General

**Show guidance under the buy button** — The feature's on/off switch (default ON).

## Shipping

**Shipping cost shown in purchase guidance** — Enter the shipping fee shown in the guidance.

<Warning>
Even for Japanese yen, enter the amount **multiplied by 100**. Example: ¥800 is `80000`. Leave blank or 0 to hide the fee. Free-shipping conditions ("only ¥X more for free shipping") use a different setting (**Theme settings → Cart**).
</Warning>

## Payment methods

**Show icons for available payment methods** — Shows icons for the store's active payment methods (credit cards and so on) in the guidance. Add or change payment methods in Shopify's payment settings.

**Payment method note** — Enter supplementary notes for methods like convenience-store payment or bank transfer, if needed. Blank shows no note.

## Returns & exchanges

**Show a link to the return policy** — Shows a link in the guidance only when a return policy is registered for the store. The return policy itself is registered in the Shopify admin under **Settings → Policies**.
