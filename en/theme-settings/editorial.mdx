## What photos & spacing is

A layout setting that switches the look of the entire store at once. Turning on the **editorial layout** applies airy navigation, full-bleed photo display, and editorial heading typography. Colors and fonts continue to use the existing theme settings.

In the theme editor, open **Theme settings → Photos & spacing**.

## Settings

**Use the editorial layout** — This checkbox switches the whole layout (default OFF).

**Left and right page margins** — The width of the margins reserved on the page's left and right.

**Spacing around photos** — The margin placed around the product's main photo. At 100%, spacing is adjusted to the screen's shorter edge (product main photos fall in the 16-32px range, with equal widths on all sides). At 0%, photos display edge to edge.

<Note>
The editorial layout toggle applies store-wide. To change only specific pages, use the section-side settings where available, or consider asking a developer.
</Note>
