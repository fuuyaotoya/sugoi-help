## Favorites & color chips

![Product card close-up with color chips](/en/images/product-card-closeup.png)

In the theme editor, open **Theme settings → Favorites & color chips**.

**Favorites page** — Choose the page where the "Favorites" section ([Favorites list](/en/features/sections/sg-favorites)) is placed. When chosen, a favorites icon (heart) appears in the header. Without a page selected, the icon is not shown.

**Shape of color chips on product cards** — Choose the shape of the color chips shown on product cards in listings and elsewhere.

- **Circle** (default)
- **Square**
- **Rounded square** — Uses the button corner-radius setting
- **Don't show**

The product page side is configured separately (Theme settings → Product information → Variant picker).

**Show color names on product cards** — Shows the color name beside the color chip on product cards. Turned off, only the color marks (dots) are shown.

## Icons & motion

In the theme editor, open **Theme settings → Icons & motion**.

**Icon set** — Switch between Dawn's standard icons and SUGOI's SVG icons. When SUGOI is selected, the weight setting below becomes available too.

**SUGOI icon weight** — When using SUGOI icons, choose thin, standard, or bold. Does not apply to Dawn's standard icons.

**Icon motion** — Adds a subtle reaction to icons on buttons and elsewhere. Choose "none" to stop the motion.

**Use a fade on page transitions** — Joins navigation from product listings to product pages and back with a subtle fade. Unsupported browsers and "reduce motion" settings fall back to normal transitions.
