What Japanese ecommerce expects should not be an afterthought bolted on with apps. SUGOI builds the parts that overseas-born themes have patched over with apps directly into the theme.

![Cart page — order notes, preferred delivery date and time, and the free-shipping bar](/images/phase3-cart.png)

## Gift wrapping (noshi), inscriptions, and packaging

Gift wrapping (*noshi* — the traditional folded paper attached to Japanese gifts) is part of the order flow. Shoppers choose the inscription (a thank-you, a celebratory gift, and so on), whether the noshi is placed inside or outside the wrapping (inner *noshi* / outer *noshi*), and the packaging type as they order. Gifting can be turned on or off per product.

- In the cart's gift options area, shoppers specify the noshi type, the inscription, and the name imprinting
- Everything is recorded as order attributes in Shopify's order data, so it is visible in the admin and in CSV exports
- For products that do not support gifting, the corresponding UI is automatically hidden

## Preferred delivery date and time

Calendar-based date selection and time-slot choice come standard in the cart — ready to absorb seasonal products and gift-driven demand.

## Visible shipping costs

An "only ¥X to free shipping" bar resolves shipping-cost anxiety — a major cause of cart abandonment — before it becomes a problem.

## Showing delivery and shipping fees

The theme ships with standard flows that surface "roughly when will it arrive if I order now" and "how much is shipping" on the product page and in the cart, up front. To present delivery and fee information in one table, use the [Shipping & fees section](/en/features/sections/sg-shipping-info); preferred delivery date and time in the cart can be added through the Japanese ecommerce essentials settings.

On stores running SUGOI Chat, delivery-timing questions asked in the chat also get answers consistent with these displays ([SUGOI Chat integration](/en/integrations/sugoi-chat)).

## Large variant matrices

Even variant configurations using the full 2,048 option combinations keep the product page intact. Catalogs heavy on size × color × spec combinations go up as they are.

## Specified Commercial Transactions Act disclosures

The legally required disclosure page — seller information, return policy, and the rest of Japan's consumer protection disclosures — is provided by the theme itself, styled cleanly even when long content would otherwise overflow.
