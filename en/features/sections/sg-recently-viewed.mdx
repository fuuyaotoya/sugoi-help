A section that automatically lists the products the shopper viewed recently. Viewing history is stored in the shopper's browser, so it works without login. Product cards appear from history, newest first, up to the configured count.

![The recently viewed section, shown once the customer has browsing history](/en/images/sg-recently-viewed.png)

## How to add it

Open the theme editor (in the admin: "Online store → Themes → Customize"), and on the page where you want it, choose **"Recently viewed"** from "Add section". It can be added to any page type. Proven on journeys where shoppers resume browsing, such as the favorites page.

## Settings

- **Heading**: The section heading
- **Number of products to show**: The maximum number of products shown (default 6)
- **Desktop columns**: Products per row on desktop (default 4)
- **Mobile columns**: Columns on smartphones, "1" or "2" (default 2)
- **Image aspect ratio**: Choose the product image ratio from "Portrait / Square / Adapt to image" (default: portrait)
- **Show second image on hover**: Hovering a card swaps to the product's second image (default ON)
- **Color scheme**: The section's color scheme

<Note>
History is recorded the moment a shopper views a product page. Shoppers with no history yet see this section hidden — never an empty frame.
</Note>
