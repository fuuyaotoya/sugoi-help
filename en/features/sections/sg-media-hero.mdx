title: Media Hero
description: A slide-based hero section with large photos or video. Multiple slides with overlaid text and buttons, plus point highlights directly beneath the hero
---

A hero section for the very top of a page, laying out large photos or video with a catchline and buttons overlaid. Multiple slides can rotate, and up to three points can be arranged directly beneath the hero.

## How to add it

Open the theme editor (in the admin: "Online store → Themes → Customize"), and on the page where you want it, choose **"Media hero"** from "Add section". It can be added to any page type (except header and footer areas). Proven on home pages.

## Settings

- **Color scheme**: The color scheme for the whole section
- **Desktop height**: The hero's height on desktop (default 67)
- **Mobile photo height**: The hero's height on smartphones (default 36)
- **Slowly animate the background**: Adds a slow parallax motion to the photo
- **Auto-rotate slides**: When there are multiple slides, automatically advances to the next
- **Seconds between transitions**: The auto-advance interval in seconds (default 6)

## Blocks

### Slide (limit 5)

One screen's worth of hero content. The photo and text arrangement can differ per slide.

- **Layout**: Place the photo on the right or left, or use it as a full-bleed background (default: photo right)
- **Desktop image**: Choose the photo used on desktop
- **Desktop image position**: Choose the photo's position within its area. "Focal point set on the image" uses the focal point saved in the image editor. Direction choices reflect in the preview immediately
- **Mobile image**: Choose the photo used on smartphones (falls back to the desktop photo when unset)
- **Mobile image position**: The photo's position on smartphone displays
- **Video uploaded to Shopify**: Shows a video instead of a photo
- **Image display**: Choose "Show entire image" (letterboxed) or "Fill the image area" (cropped to fit) (default: show entire image)
- **Extra padding around the photo**: Adjusts the inset padding inside the photo area (default 100)
- **Photo frame decoration**: Adds a decorative frame to the photo
- **Small heading**: Small text shown above the heading (e.g., DENIM, DECODED.)
- **Heading**: The slide's main heading
- **Heading level**: Choose the heading's HTML level, H1 or H2 (default H2). For the first hero on a page, H1 is appropriate
- **Body**: The slide's descriptive text
- **Button label**: The main button's text
- **Button link**: Where the main button links
- **Secondary link label**: The text of the secondary link placed beside the main button
- **Secondary link**: Where the secondary link goes
- **Small footnote**: A small note shown at the bottom of the slide
- **Vertical text beside the photo**: Text displayed vertically alongside the photo

### Points below the hero (limit 3)

Short points displayed side by side directly beneath the hero.

- **Point heading**: The point's title (e.g., Free shipping nationwide)
- **Point body**: The point's description

<Note>
Set the first slide's heading level to H1 and leave the second and later slides at H2 — this keeps the page's heading structure correct, which benefits both SEO and accessibility.
</Note>
