A section that combines images and prose to tell the story behind a brand or product, or how to choose it. Numbered steps can be inserted into the body, and the section closes with a quotation.

## How to add it

Open the theme editor (in the admin: "Online store → Themes → Customize"), and on the page where you want it, choose **"Editorial story"** from "Add section". It can be added to any page type (except header and footer areas). Proven on home pages.

## Settings

- **Image**: The main image
- **Second image**: With a second image, the two sit side by side for a richer composition (one image alone also works)
- **Eyebrow**: A small label shown above the heading (default: Hints for choosing)
- **Heading**: The section heading (default: Choose the one you're sure of)
- **Body**: The story's body text (rich text)
- **Link label**: The text of the link beneath the body (default: Learn more)
- **Link**: The destination URL
- **Closing eyebrow**: A small label shown above the closing quote
- **Closing words**: The words displayed as a quotation at the end of the section
- **Section color scheme**: The color scheme around the body
- **Closing words color scheme**: The quote's color scheme (default scheme-2. Changing it adds contrast to the close)
- **Top / Bottom**: Padding above and below the section (default 64 / 64)

## Blocks

### Step

A numbered step inserted into the body. There is no limit on the number. Use them to explain product selection or care instructions in order.

- **Number**: The number shown for the step (e.g., 1)
- **Text**: The step's description

<Note>
With no images set, the section automatically switches to a text-only layout. It renders without breaking even before images are ready.
</Note>
