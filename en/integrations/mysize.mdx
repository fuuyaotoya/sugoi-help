The My Size feature of the **SUGOI Customer Account** app eliminates the "which size was I again?" moment. Once shoppers save their size, every product page they open afterwards is displayed with that size already selected.

## How it works

1. The shopper saves their size on the account page (or during a chat size consultation)
2. Opening a product page with a saved size **pre-selects** the matching variant
3. A chip such as "**Saved size M applied**" appears on screen, preventing mis-orders

The saved size is shared across products. Products with different size scales (apparel S/M/L, shoe sizes in centimeters, and so on) get it applied automatically whenever a matching option exists.

## Designed for safety

- Application stops at pre-selection and the chip display. The final purchase decision remains the shopper's own action
- Out-of-stock and unavailable variants are never auto-selected — availability is checked against the full variant matrix
- Sizes are stored in the browser and never sent elsewhere without consent

## Working with chat

A size settled during a [SUGOI Chat](/en/integrations/sugoi-chat) consultation is saved as My Size, with the shopper's consent. On every visit afterwards, product pages open with that size already selected — the store remembers the size decided in the last chat.

## Roadmap

- **Visible, self-editable sizes** — view and edit your saved size on the account page
- **Chat from order details** — one tap takes order-status questions straight to chat
- **In-account concierge** — use AI consulting directly from your account page

<Note>
  My Size integration requires the SUGOI Customer Account app. The theme is unaffected without it (absent the integration, manual selection is always the standard behavior).
</Note>
