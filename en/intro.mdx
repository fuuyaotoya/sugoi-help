SUGOI Theme is a theme pack for Japanese ecommerce, built on Shopify's official **Dawn** theme and delivered with its composition tailored to the shape of your trade — wagashi and food, fashion, automotive parts. Gift wrapping (*noshi*), preferred delivery date and time, and even an AI chat concierge are built in from the start.

## Industry presets

Rather than assembling a generic theme yourself, you receive a theme already composed for your industry. Stores for each industry ship from the same foundation.

## Why SUGOI

### Built directly on Shopify's official lineage

SUGOI extends Dawn — the world's most widely used official Shopify theme — without altering a single byte. Compatibility with Shopify apps and alignment with checkout and platform features are never compromised by the theme.

### Industry presets — shipped lean

Sections and translations your industry never uses are removed before delivery, so the Theme Editor stays uncluttered and pages stay light. SUGOI handles the curation, so you can focus on running your store.

### A stocked inventory of 30+ custom sections

Vertical-text heroes, opening titles, limited drops, style quizzes, recently viewed, quick view, comparison and spec tables — we maintain a growing inventory of finished components that can be combined to fit each project. Product page compositions can be chosen to match each product, too.

### Machine-verified quality, before delivery

We ship only when the theme passes Shopify's official theme check with zero errors. On top of that, E2E tests across three browser engines and WCAG accessibility checks run automatically. What you receive is not "probably works" — it is "verified."

## AI chat concierge

After hours, the chat greets visitors the way a staff member would. SUGOI's chat is not decoration — it is a concierge that resolves the three points where shopping stalls (size, gifting, and delivery) through conversation. Whatever is settled in conversation carries straight into the cart.

For details, see the [SUGOI Chat integration guide](/en/integrations/sugoi-chat).

## How onboarding works

1. **Discovery** — We start by listening to the shape of your trade: the nature of your products (fresh, made-to-order, size ranges), whether you offer gifts, and your seasonal waves. All of this informs the composition.
2. **Composition** — Working from an industry preset, we propose the section combination and the product page composition. At this point, you can already see what the store will look like.
3. **Build and verification** — After building with your real product data, we finish the release candidate through automated tests, accessibility checks, and hands-on device verification.
4. **Delivery and beyond** — Delivery is not the end. We continue to provide translation additions, seasonal sections, and feature improvements. The theme is something we keep growing.
