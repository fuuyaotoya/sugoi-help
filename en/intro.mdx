## Works on its own. Even better together.

SUGOI Theme is built on Shopify's official **Dawn** theme, with the features Japanese ecommerce needs included from the start.

**On its own, it works as a store immediately.** Even without any AI apps, the shopping flow Japanese commerce requires — gift wrapping (*noshi*), preferred delivery date and time, delivery estimates, and Specified Commercial Transactions Act disclosures — is all there from day one.

Add SUGOI's apps on top, and the theme awakens into an **AI store**.

## The basic setup flow

1. **Apply the theme** — add the theme files to your store and publish
2. **Review theme settings** — configure the SUGOI groups in theme settings (gift, delivery, AI chat, and more) to match your store → [Theme settings guide](/en/theme-settings)
3. **Lay out your content** — arrange sections in the Theme Editor and build each page → [Section reference](/en/features/sections/sg-media-hero)
4. **(Optional) Add the apps** — install SUGOI Chat / SUGOI Account and awaken the store → [Integration guide](/en/integrations/sugoi-chat)
